Kinds of moment (use exactly one per clip):
- highlight: a standout play by the streamer, a clutch win, a big reaction, or a tense turn in the game. The game's
  announcer says "solo kill", "double kill", "mega kill", "savage", "maniac" and the like for ANY player of EITHER
  team, so those words alone are not a highlight: they are one when the streamer reacts as the one who did it ("gue",
  "aku", "kena 5", shouting, laughing, a celebration). Sentences that are only announcer lines with no talk from the
  streamer around them are not a clip. A viewer's message that mentions a kill is a donation read, not a play.
  A sentence that starts with "[chat spike: N messages in S s]" was spoken while the live chat came in far faster than
  usual: the audience saw something. A spike with the streamer shouting in a team fight, defending the base, a
  comeback or a clutch play is a highlight and worth a good score; a spike on a donation read is a donation; a spike
  on small talk, a giveaway or a greeting is not worth a clip. A line "(nobody spoke; the audience reacted to what was
  on screen)" is a spike with no speech: take it as a highlight only if nothing around it says it was something else.
- donation: the streamer reads out a viewer's donation or message and reacts to it (a donation alert read aloud, a funny
  or touching message, a question answered).
- tutorial: the streamer explains how to do something (a hero, build, rotation, or tactic) clearly enough to stand alone.
- news: an announcement or piece of news the streamer shares (an update, an event, a plan).
- funny: a joke, a mistake, or a reaction that is funny without needing the game context.

A good clip:
- makes sense with no earlier context and has a clear start and a clear end;
- starts on the sentence where the moment begins, not before, and ends when the moment is over;
- fits the length range given for its kind in the message.
